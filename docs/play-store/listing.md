## Full description (max 4000)

```text
NavGesture adds edge gestures to your phone. Swipe in from a side for Back, swipe up from the bottom for Home, and pause before letting go for Recent apps. It works with any launcher and needs no root.

GESTURES
• Swipe in from the left or right edge: Back
• Swipe up from the bottom edge: Home
• Swipe up, pause, then let go: Recent apps
• Swipe up, then drag right: a row of your recent apps. Each short drag moves one app; lift to open it
• Swipe up, then drag left: a row of your favourite apps
• Swipe down while the row is open: close it
• Touch and hold the middle of the bottom edge: Circle to Search (optional)

A small drop rises from the edge under your finger and shows what will happen before you let go. Every distance and delay is adjustable, and you can turn on haptic feedback.

WHY PEOPLE USE IT
On some phones, gesture navigation only works with the phone maker's own launcher. Switch to Nova, Niagara or Lawnchair and you are back to three buttons. NavGesture gives the gestures back, with the launcher you like.

PRIVACY
• No ads, no account, no analytics.
• No internet permission: this version cannot send anything anywhere.
• Nothing it reads is saved off your phone or included in cloud backups.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
NavGesture uses Android's Accessibility service to read the position and timing of touches inside thin strips along the screen edges, and to perform Back, Home and Recents. It is configured so it cannot read what is on your screen or what you type. Touches that are not a gesture are passed on to the app underneath. The app explains this and asks for your consent before you turn the service on.

USAGE ACCESS (OPTIONAL)
If you grant Usage access, the app switcher lists the apps you opened recently. That list stays on your phone.

GESTURE NAVIGATION PHONES
On phones that use Android's own gesture navigation, the phone itself may handle slow side swipes, the bottom swipe-and-hold (Recents) and the app row. Back and Home keep working either way. Switch to three-button navigation in your phone's settings to get every NavGesture gesture.

OPEN SOURCE
NavGesture is free software under the GPL-3.0 licence. Source code: https://github.com/vijayyaso/NavGesture

Works on Android 11 and newer.
```
